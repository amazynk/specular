Text files open in a text view. Specular never changes them.

## Text and logs {#text}
- Every line shows with its number, even in logs of many gigabytes
- Wrap lines: the button above the text
- Unwrapped lines: Shift and the wheel scroll sideways
- NFO and DIZ files show their DOS art in its own characters

The character set is found by itself. The info panel names it.

## Markdown {#markdown}
Markdown shows formatted, as pages.

Show source switches to the text; Show formatted switches back.

Pictures load only from files next to the document. Links show but do not open.

## Tables {#tables}
CSV and TSV files show as a grid. The first row stays on top when it names the columns.

Shift and the wheel move across the columns.

## RTF {#rtf}
RTF documents show formatted, as pages, with their tables and pictures.

## Subtitles {#subtitles}
A subtitle file shows its cues, each with its start and end time.

SRT, WebVTT, ASS, SSA, MicroDVD, LRC, TTML, DFXP and SCC show words; VobSub and PGS show pictures.

A VobSub `.idx` needs its `.sub` beside it.

To show subtitles over a video, see [Subtitles](video#subtitles).
