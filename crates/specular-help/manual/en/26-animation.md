Animated GIF, WebP, PNG, AVIF and JPEG XL images play in the viewer, as do ANI cursors and FLI/FLC animations.

## Playback {#playback}
- [[key:play-pause]]: play or pause
- [[key:next-frame]] and [[key:prev-frame]]: step one frame
- [[key:faster]], [[key:slower]] and [[key:normal-speed]] change the speed
- [[key:loop-forever]]: loop forever, whatever the file says

Playback commands are unavailable for still images.

For videos, see [Videos](video).
