A font file opens as a specimen page in its own typeface.

The page shows the font's names, its licence, a sample at several sizes and every glyph.

## Using the specimen {#specimen}
- Scroll: the wheel
- Variable fonts: drag a slider to change weight, width or another axis
- Glyphs: the arrows above the grid show the next or previous glyphs
- Collections (TTC, OTC): each font is a page; [[key:next-page]] and [[key:prev-page]] step through them

## Colour fonts {#colour}
Colour layers (COLR version 0) and colour bitmaps show in a row of their own.

Other colour glyphs (COLR version 1, SVG) show in one colour; a line on the page says so.

## Info panel {#info}
The info panel lists glyphs, tables, outline type and what the licence allows when the font is embedded in documents.
