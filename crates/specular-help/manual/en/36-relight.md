RTI and PTM files record an object under light from many directions. Moving the light reveals faint detail, such as worn inscriptions.

Relighting commands are unavailable for ordinary images.

## Moving the light {#light}
Drag over the image, or use the keys:

- [[key:light-left]], [[key:light-right]], [[key:light-up]], [[key:light-down]] move the light
- [[key:light-widget]]: dial showing the light's direction
- [[key:light-reset]]: light back overhead

## Shading {#shading}
- [[key:relight-shading]]: object's own colours or shading that shows surface shape
