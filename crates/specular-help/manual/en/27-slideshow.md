Show a folder as a slideshow, on this screen or another.

## Slideshow {#slideshow}
Press [[key:slideshow]] to start or stop it.

Settings › Slideshow sets the time per image, order, transition and caption.

## Presenting on another display {#present}
[[key:viewer-on-other-monitor]] moves the image to another display, such as a projector. This window stays free for choosing what to show.

With only one display, Specular tells you so.

With a second display, the slideshow runs there. This window shows Pause and Stop. Settings › Slideshow › Display picks the display.

## Saving as a video {#export}
[[key:export-slideshow]] saves the slideshow as a video file.
