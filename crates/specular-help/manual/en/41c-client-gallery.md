A client gallery is a folder with a web page and your photos. Open it from disk or put it on any web host.

1. Select photos in the [gallery](gallery), or open an album or a search.
2. Press [[key:client-gallery]].
3. Choose the title, photos, theme, size and format.
4. Choose **Write gallery…** and a folder.

The gallery goes in a new folder named after its title. Nothing is replaced.

Photos go out with their Develop edits.

**Watermark text** is drawn on every photo.

**Under each photo** shows the file name, the title or the caption.

**Show camera and exposure** adds camera, lens, exposure and date in the viewer.

GPS is removed from the photos. Turn on **Keep location (GPS) in the photos** to keep it.

The page loads nothing from the internet. No fonts, no tracking.

## Passwords

A static gallery cannot be protected by a password.

Anyone with the folder or its address sees every photo.

Share the link only with your client, or use your web host's access control.

## Client picks {#client-picks}

Your client taps the heart on the photos they want. Picks stay in their browser.

They send them back with **Copy my picks**, or with the downloaded `picks.txt` or `picks.json`.

1. Press [[key:import-client-picks]].
2. Paste their list, or choose their file.
3. Choose how to mark them: as picks, with stars or with a label.
4. Check how many matched, and which names were not found.
5. Choose **Apply**.

Names match the photos the gallery lists, by file name.

A name found in two folders is left alone.

Ctrl+Z undoes the marks.
