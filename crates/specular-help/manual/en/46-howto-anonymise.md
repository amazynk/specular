title: How do I share a study without personal details?

---
1. Open the study.
2. Press [[key:export-anonymised]] to anonymise it.
3. Check the list of what will be removed or replaced: names, identifiers, dates of birth, addresses and similar details.
4. Choose a folder and save.

Dates can be shifted instead of removed. This keeps the time between scans.

Some images have names written into the pixels, such as ultrasound screens. Press [[key:mask-burned-in-text]] to cover that text, and check the result before sharing.

> Anonymised copies are new files. The original study is not changed.
