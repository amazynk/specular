1. Open the folder in the [gallery](gallery). Specular looks for duplicates in the background.
2. Choose **Duplicates** or **Similar shots** in the View menu or the palette. Each shows its groups and the space to free.
3. Review each group. Specular suggests which to keep, with a reason.
4. Discard the others: to the trash (the default), into a folder, or only flagged as rejected.

**Accept all suggestions** does every group at once. It asks first.

You can cancel the scan at any time. Groups found so far are kept, and running it again carries on where it stopped.

Nothing is moved or deleted until you choose to.
