Convert saves copies. The originals are never changed.

## Converting {#steps}
1. Select images in the [gallery](gallery), or open one in the viewer.
2. Press [[key:convert]], or choose **Convert…** in the palette.
3. Choose the format, quality and, optionally, a new size.
4. Choose where the copies go and how they are named.
5. Choose **Convert**.

The dialog shows the new file sizes before you start.

Conversion runs in the background and can be stopped. Files already written are kept.

## Metadata {#metadata}
Copies keep the Exif and XMP details, location included. The colour profile is always kept.

Keywords given in Specular go with them. Private keywords stay out.

> To leave out the location, or all details, use Settings › Convert › Metadata. Settings › Convert also holds the dialog's defaults.
