Paged documents, books, comic archives and folders of numbered scans read like books.

Documents: PDF, DjVu, XPS, OpenXPS, CGM, EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3) and FB2.

## Pages {#pages}
- [[key:next-page]] and [[key:prev-page]]: turn the page
- [[key:first-page]] and [[key:last-page]]: go to the start or end
- [[key:spreads]]: facing pages side by side, cover alone
- [[key:right-to-left]]: turn pages right to left, as in manga
- [[key:zoom-fit-width]] and [[key:zoom-fit-height]]: fit each page to the window's width or height

Page commands are unavailable for a file with one page.

## Archives {#archives}
Comic archives (CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT) and other archives open as books, pages in natural order.

A book reopens at the page last read.

ComicInfo.xml can set manga reading order; [[key:right-to-left]] overrides it.

Images inside an archive cannot be moved to the trash or renamed, because they are not separate files. Extract the archive first to change them.

## Office documents {#office}
- Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers, Keynote and OpenDocument files show the preview picture saved in them.
- The document itself is not drawn.
- [[key:info]]: the title, author, dates and page or slide counts.
- A file saved without a preview says so. Its details are still in the info panel.
- Password-protected Word, Excel and PowerPoint files do not open.

## EPUB books {#epub}
A book opens at its cover. Its text is laid out in pages of the same size on every computer.

- [[key:contents]]: the table of contents; click an entry to go there

Fixed-layout books show each page as designed.

Books locked with DRM do not open; the message names the lock. Fonts hidden by the publisher's font obfuscation are restored.

Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3), Mobipocket (PRC) and FictionBook (FB2, FB2.ZIP) books open the same way.

Kindle books with DRM do not open. Kindle KFX books do not open: the format is not published.
