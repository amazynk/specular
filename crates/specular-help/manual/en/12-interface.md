The controls fade while you look. Move the pointer or press a key to bring them back.

## The toolbar {#toolbar}
The toolbar at the bottom holds the most used commands. Hover over a button to see what it does and its key.

To choose the buttons, press [[key:customise-toolbar]] or right-click the toolbar.

The Discord button at the end opens the Specular Discord server in your browser. About has it too.

## The command palette {#palette}
Press [[key:palette]] and type a few letters of any command or setting. Matches are listed with their keys.

Commands that cannot run right now are greyed out with the reason. Choose one to read about it in this manual.

Type **help** and a word to search the manual from the palette.

::: commands

## The cheat sheet {#cheat-sheet}
Press [[key:cheat-sheet]] to see every key that works with what is on screen. It has a tab for the viewer, the gallery, compare, cull mode and each mode your profiles add.

## The info panel {#info}
Press [[key:info]] to show file details, camera settings and other metadata beside the image.

You can copy any value. A photo with a location can be shown on a map.

## Full screen and themes {#window}
- [[key:fullscreen]]: fills the screen with the image.
- [[key:theme]]: switches between light and dark. To follow your system, use Settings › General.
- [[key:language]]: chooses the language.
- [[key:close]] or the window's close button: closes the image or folder and goes back to the start screen; on the start screen, quits. [[key:quit]] always quits.

## Downloads {#downloads}
While Specular fetches from a server you set up (remote images, Zarr stores, map tiles, DICOMweb, OMERO, Local AI models, the update check), it shows what is downloading.

With room, it shows beside the toolbar or in the gallery's bottom bar: what, how much, how fast, time left and a progress bar.

Without room, a small download arrow stands in. Click it, or reach it with Tab, to open the **Downloads** panel.

The panel lists each download with its figures. **Stop** cancels one.

Only the server's name is shown, never a full address or a password. Nothing shows when nothing is downloading.

## Help {#help}
Press [[key:help]] to open this manual at the page for what you are doing.

In the manual, search as you type, follow links, and press Alt+Left to go back.
