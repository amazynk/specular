AI features are optional and run only on this computer. No photo, face or text they find ever leaves it.

## Features {#features}
- **Search by description**: find photos by describing them, such as “dog on a beach”, in many languages. Type `like:` and a description, or plain words.
- **Text in images**: read text in photos and scans, copy it, and find photos by the words in them.
- **Culling assistance**: flag closed eyes, blur and poor exposure to pick the best shot of a burst.
- **People**: find faces and group them by person. Grouping is off until you turn it on for a library. Delete face data for one person, or **Delete all face data**; your photos stay.
- **AI masks**: Subject, Sky and Object at click mask parts in Develop. See [AI masks](develop#ai-masks).
- **AI denoise**: removes noise in Develop, mixed with the classic result. See [AI denoise](develop#ai-denoise).
- **AI remove**: fills a brushed area in Develop's Retouch card. See [Remove (AI)](develop#ai-remove).
- **Depth**: the depth map for Depth blur, Relight and depth masks. See [Depth](develop#depth).
- **Speech to text**: subtitles from what is said in a video, in its language or translated to English. Whisper base, small or large-v3 turbo. See [Generated subtitles](video#generated-subtitles).

## People {#people}
People finds faces with BlazeFace and tells people apart with SFace (default) or AuraFace.

1. Download People in Settings › Local AI.
2. Turn on **Group faces into people** for the library. It is off until you do.
3. Specular reads the library's photos in the background and groups the faces.

Open **People** from the palette in the gallery:

- **Enter** or a double click shows the person's photos in the Library.
- **F2** names a person. Search for them with `person:` and the name.
- An unnamed person is shown as “Person #12”; `person:#12` finds them.
- **Space** selects people; **M** merges them into the focused one. You are asked first.
- Or drag a person onto another to merge them. The same question is asked.
- In the question, **Enter** merges, **Esc** keeps them apart, **Tab** moves between the buttons.
- **F** shows a person's faces. **Delete** takes a face from them; it is not grouped back.

Faces on their side or upside down are found too, and their boxes are drawn turned.

**Delete all face data** in Settings › Local AI removes every face and person. Your photos stay.

SFace was trained on face sets licensed for research only. AuraFace's publisher says its data allows commercial use but does not name it.

## Culling assistance {#culling}
Culling uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker for closed eyes. Blur and exposure need no model.

In cull mode the strip shows flags under the photo's details:

- **Eyes closed**, or how many faces have them closed.
- **Blurred**.
- **Dark**, **Bright**, or **Clipped highlights or shadows**.

A wink does not count as closed eyes.

## Show faces {#faces}
**Show faces** in the palette draws the faces found on the picture.

- People's faces carry the person's name.
- Faces with closed eyes are marked.

## AI masks {#masks}
The Masks feature finds an object where you click, with EfficientSAM Ti (default) or S.
- Subject (IS-Net) and Sky (UperNet ConvNeXt-T) are add-ons. Download each on its own.
- EfficientSAM and UperNet were trained on data licensed for research. The model sheet says so.
- Masks run on the photo when a mask part asks. They are kept in the cache folder, not the library.
- Removing the feature deletes its model files. Your photos and edits stay.

## AI denoise {#denoise}
The Denoise feature removes noise with FFDNet (3.4 MB).
- It is told the photo's noise level, which Develop measures. It needs no settings.
- It was trained on images licensed for research. The model sheet says so.
- It runs at 100% view and in exports, never on thumbnails.
- Results are kept in the cache folder, up to 3 GB, oldest removed first.
- Removing the feature deletes its model files. Your photos and edits stay.
## AI remove and Depth {#remove-depth}
AI remove fills brushed areas with Big-LaMa. Depth finds depth with Depth Anything V2 Small.
- Both are Apache-2.0. Their training photos are licensed for research. The model sheet says so.
- Only the small Depth Anything model is offered: the larger ones are non-commercial.
- They run on the photo when asked. Results are kept in the cache folder, not the library.
- Removing a feature deletes its model files. Your photos and edits stay.

## Turning them on {#setup}
1. Open Settings › Local AI.
2. Pick the model under the feature's **Model** setting, or keep the default.
3. Press **Download**.

Nothing is downloaded until you choose it.

Each model shows its download size, licence, training data and the site it downloads from. When its training data says terms not stated, the publisher does not say under which terms it was used.

Search uses SigLIP 2 B/16 by default. SigLIP 2 B/32 indexes about four times faster, for slower graphics cards. Each downloads 1.5 GB; Specular converts it to half the size after checking it.

PE Core S16 understands English descriptions only. Its download is 349 MB.

## Text in images {#find-text}
Press [[key:find-text]], or choose **Find text** in the palette, to read the text in the picture shown.

- Each line found is outlined on the picture with its words.
- Choose a line in the list to copy it. **Copy all text** copies every line, top to bottom.
- Hebrew and Arabic lines read right to left, as written.
- Upside-down lines are turned and read the right way up.

PP-OCRv6 small reads Chinese, Japanese and Latin script; PP-OCRv6 tiny is faster, without Japanese.

For other scripts, download an add-on under Text's **Model** setting: Arabic, Korean, Cyrillic, East Slavic, Latin, Greek, Thai, Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, and Hebrew (Tesseract). Each line is read by every installed model; the surest reading is kept.

To find photos by their text, turn on **Read text in this library’s photos** under Text's **Model** setting. It is off until you turn it on for a library. Then search the Library with `text:`, such as `text:exit` or `text:"open daily"`.

Adding or removing an add-on re-reads only the photos it matters to.

## Without internet {#offline}
For computers without internet, copy the model files over by hand:

1. Open Settings › Local AI.
2. Choose **Install from file…** or **Install from folder…**.
3. Press **Download** next to the feature. It installs from the copied files.

Each file is checked against the list of models shipped with Specular. Other files are refused.

A file with a known name but an unknown checksum is used only if you confirm **Use unverified model**.

Administrators can place model files in a folder instead; see [Privacy and the network](privacy#managed).
