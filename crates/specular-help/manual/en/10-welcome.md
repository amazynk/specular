Specular shows photos, scans, comics, medical studies, scientific data, drawings, maps and 3D models. It never changes your files; anything it makes, such as a converted copy or a print, is a new file.

Type in the search field above to find a topic. Press [[key:help]] anywhere to open the page for what is on screen.

## Where to begin {#begin}
- [Opening files](opening): open a file, a folder or the clipboard.
- [The window](interface): toolbar, command palette, cheat sheet and info panel.
- [Profiles](profiles): turn on tools for medicine, science or engineering.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](keys): every key, as you have set them.

## Viewing and browsing {#modes}
- [The viewer](viewer): one image; zoom, pan, rotate, step through a folder.
- [The gallery](gallery): a folder as thumbnails.
- [The library](library): all photos in your library folders, searchable.
- [Compare](compare) and [cull mode](cull): choose between photos.
- [Reading mode](reading): comics, scanned books and other paged documents.

## Special kinds of images {#domains}
- [Medical images](medical) and [medical 3D](medical-3d): DICOM studies.
- [Scientific images](science): astronomy, microscopy and other data.
- [Maps and geodata](maps), [drawings](drawing) and [3D models](mesh): engineering and GIS.
- [Relightable images](relight): objects photographed under moving light.

## Your privacy {#privacy}
Specular collects no data about you and has no telemetry. It uses the network only when you ask it to.

See [Privacy and the network](privacy).
