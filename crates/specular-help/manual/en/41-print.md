1. Open the image, or select several in the [gallery](gallery).
2. Press [[key:print]].
3. Choose the paper, layout (one per page or contact sheet) and size.
4. Choose **Print**.

The preview shows the pages exactly as they will print.

In a right-to-left language, contact sheets start at the top right and the title is on the right.

Colours match the printer's profile when your system provides one.

## Free layouts

1. Set **Layout** to **Free layout**.
2. Pick a **Saved layout**, or **Add area** and place it.
3. Drag an area to move it, its handles to resize it.
4. Shift+drag a filled photo to move it inside its area.

Photos fill the areas in the order you selected them.

Edges snap to the margins, the centre and the other areas. **Snap** turns on a grid as well.

Type margins and gaps in mm or inches. Units follow your region.

Each area shows its printed size and the photo's resolution. Below 200 dpi it is marked as low.

**Save layout…** keeps the layout by name. **Delete layout** asks first.

**Borderless** appears only when the printer reports it. Some printers crop a little off the edges.

## From Develop

**Style** applies a style to the printed copy only. Your edit stays as it is.

**Black point compensation** is on for relative colorimetric. It keeps detail in the shadows.

**Soft proof** shows the preview as the printer will print it.
