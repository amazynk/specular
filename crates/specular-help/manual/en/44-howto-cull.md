1. Open the shoot's folder or memory card.
2. Press [[key:cull]] to enter [cull mode](cull).
3. Press [[key:cull-pick]] to keep an image or [[key:cull-reject]] to reject it. The next image follows.
4. In a burst, press [[key:cull-keep-best]] to keep the suggested best frame.
5. In the [gallery](gallery), show only the picks and copy or move them.

Your editor can read the marks too. They are saved in XMP sidecar files next to the images.
